Frequently asked.

Can I place a real order?

Not yet. This is a demonstration store. Checkout does not send an order or take payment.

How does demo delivery work?

The cart shows an illustrative R25 delivery charge, or free delivery on orders of R150 or more. No delivery will actually be made.

Can I choose my own six-pack?

Yes. Choose any six cans in the six-pack builder. Individual can prices apply.

Are the product details final?

No. Fizzly is a placeholder brand. Prices, descriptions and availability are for demonstration only.

How do I contact Fizzly?

A contact channel hasn’t been set up for this concept yet.
